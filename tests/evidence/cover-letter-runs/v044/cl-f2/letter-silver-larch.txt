Dear Silver Larch School District hiring team,

Your new curriculum will give your teachers new lessons, and common unit assessments can show them which students those lessons miss before they fall behind.

In 2019 I became department chair and started moving our department toward common unit tests, so every math teacher in the department would look at the same evidence. We made the switch in fall 2021. That winter our teachers started meeting every week to go over the results, and every three weeks they regrouped students by the latest unit tests.

From 2022 to 2024 I also led monthly professional development for 18 math teachers from three middle schools in our district. Each of those Basalt Creek sessions covered how to read unit test results and how to plan reteach lessons from them. Your 30 math teachers would get sessions like those, and I'd coach your new math teachers in their own classrooms on reading their students' unit tests and planning the reteach lessons that follow.
