## 3. The hiring team's view

We came away thinking Graham gets the groundwork done before the work reaches the street. At Harlow Valley he started the lining program, wrote its specifications and ran a meeting for the residents before each street was lined, and his lining drew far fewer complaints per mile than open-trench work did. He runs his grant projects the same way, from a bid calendar that works back from each grant deadline. Our new lining group needs that habit, because we've hired out all of our lining until now, and whoever sets the group up will decide how we specify the work and how we treat the streets it runs through.

Our concern was how recent his lining work is. It comes from his years as a project engineer, and since he moved into his current job, his record has been about getting grant-funded projects to bid. What answered it was that Harlow Valley still uses the lining specifications he wrote, and his current job still covers sewer projects as well as water. He also lined more sewer main there than our first target.

We'd hire him to set up the lining group and lead its first projects, and we'd expect his first months to go into learning our sewer system and the contractors who've done our lining so far.

## 4. The case in four lines

1. **What they want:** A lead who sets up Calder Basin's new trenchless lining group and lines its first ten miles of sewer main, in an authority whose lining has all been done by contractors so far. The authority will also talk with every finalist about its other program, which has to get grant-funded water mains to bid by June 2027.
2. **Where the candidate stands** (a working note, never on a page): Strong on the lining job itself, since he has set up a lining program, written its specifications and lined more than ten miles of sewer main. That work dates from 2017 to 2020, and his record since then is grant-funded projects taken to bid, which is also strong for the other program but smaller than its $40 million. No source shows him managing a construction contract after award or reporting to a board.
3. **The story the evidence tells:** At Harlow Valley Utilities he started a trenchless sewer lining program, wrote its specifications, met the residents of each street before it was lined, and had lined 14 miles of main by 2020 at about 40% of the cost of open-trench replacement.
4. **What the reader should believe:** Graham has set up a sewer lining program and lined more sewer main than Calder Basin's first target, and he can do the same for the authority's new lining group.
