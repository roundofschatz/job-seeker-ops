## 3. The hiring team's view

We came away thinking Renata improves a shipping number by changing the routine behind it. When Tumbleweed opened its Fernley cross-dock, she built the shift schedule and trained the first crew, and on-time shipping climbed within months. Her answer to mis-picks was a second scan at pack-out, and weekly cycle counts kept the inventory accurate.

Our Sparks building needs that habit. It opened this spring and still ships too many store orders late, and our stores are running short of the gear customers come in for. She has taken a new building from worse than where we stand today to better than our goal, in about the time we've had.

Our concern was size. Fernley was a cross-dock whose first crew was smaller than one of our shifts, while ours is a full distribution center with receiving, putaway and picking under one roof. What answered it was the job she does every day. She supervises a second shift in a large distribution center, and before that she led a receiving team and planned its putaway.

We'd hire her to get a shift at Sparks shipping on time. Her first weeks would go into learning how our departments run, and she has done much of that floor work herself, as an associate and as a lead.
