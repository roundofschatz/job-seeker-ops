## 3. The hiring team's view

We came away thinking Renata solves an operations problem by changing the routine. When she opened Tumbleweed's Fernley cross-dock, she built the shift schedule and trained the first associates, and on-time shipping rose from there. As a shift supervisor, she cut mis-picks by adding a second scan at pack-out.

That fits where Sparks is now. The building is new, too many store orders ship late, and our stores are short of gear. We need a supervisor who has opened a building before and knows what to set up first, and she has.

Our concern was whether what she did at Fernley would work at Sparks. Fernley was a cross-dock, and Sparks is a bigger distribution center than the one where she runs a shift now. The rest of her record answered it. She supervises a shift in a distribution center today, she kept inventory accurate there with weekly cycle counts, and before that she picked and received freight herself, then led a receiving team.

We'd hire her to bring Sparks up to speed the way she did at Fernley. We'd expect her first months to go into learning our departments, starting with the shift in each one that every new supervisor works, and into the safety side of the job.
