## 3. The hiring team's view

We came away thinking Renata fixes a shift's numbers by changing how the work gets done. When she opened the Fernley cross-dock, she built its shift schedule and trained its first associates, and its on-time shipping rose past the goal we've set for Sparks. At Tumbleweed she cut mis-picks by adding a second scan at pack-out.

Sparks needs that habit. Every store's orders now ship from one new building that's still well short of its on-time goal, and stores are running out of the gear customers come in for. She has taken a new building from a slow start to above that goal before, in about the time Sparks has been open.

Our concern was the kind of building, because Fernley was a cross-dock and Sparks is a full distribution center, where the job covers receiving, putaway, picking and cycle counts. Her current job answered it, since she supervises second shift in a distribution center on a warehouse management system and her weekly cycle counts kept its inventory accurate. She also came up through receiving and putaway, first as an associate and then as a lead.

We'd hire her to get Sparks to its on-time goal, and we'd expect her first weeks to go into a shift in each of our departments, the way every new supervisor here starts.
