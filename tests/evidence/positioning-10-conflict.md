# Positioning test 10: conflict

Spec: "A deeper source whose date or figure differs from the resume gets caught and put to the person at step 9, with the resume's version as the default."

- Run f1, writer F. Her resume says the share of 8th graders meeting the state standard went from 44% to 61%, and her master resume says from 44% to 63%. Files: `positioning-runs/f1/`.

## What happened

Her step 9 message asked, word for word: "**61% or 63%?** The resume you're sending says the share went from 44% to 61% between 2021 and 2024. Your master resume says 63%. Which is right? If you don't say, I'll use 61%, because the resume is what they'll read. If 63% is right, fix the resume before you upload it."

She answered that 61% was right. The saved file gives her result as 61% in P1's result and story, in line 3 of the case and in the requirement map. 63% appears once, in the working note: "The master resume's 63% is a typo, and 61% stands (A3)."

## Result

Pass.
