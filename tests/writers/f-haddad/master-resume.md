# Nadia Haddad: master resume

Last updated November 2, 2025. Everything I've done, to pick from.

## Cottonwood Springs Middle School, Basalt Creek School District, Spokane, WA

### Math Department Chair, July 2019 to now

- Lead a department of nine math teachers across grades 6 to 8.
- Raised the share of 8th graders meeting the state math standard from 44% to 63% between 2021 and 2024. We moved to common unit tests in fall 2021 and started weekly data meetings that winter.
- Built common unit tests for grades 6 to 8. Teachers use them to regroup students every three weeks.
- Led monthly professional development for 18 math teachers across three Basalt Creek middle schools from 2022 to 2024. The sessions covered reading unit test results and planning reteach lessons.
- Designed and ran Summer Bridge Math in 2023, a four-week program for 120 incoming 6th graders who had scored below grade level in 5th grade. That fall, 52% of the students who attended passed the district's first math benchmark, against 38% of similar students who didn't attend.
- Served on the district's math curriculum review committee for the 2020 adoption of a new 6th grade textbook, and piloted two of the three finalists in my classes.

- Co-wrote the school's 7th grade pacing guide in 2017.
