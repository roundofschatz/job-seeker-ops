Senior Project Engineer, Capital Programs
Calder Basin Water Authority, Bend, Oregon. Full time.

This hire will lead one of two programs, and we'll shape the role around the person we choose. Our grant-funded water main replacement program has to get $40 million in projects to bid by June 2027, or the state takes the money back. Our new trenchless lining group needs someone to set it up and line its first ten miles of sewer main. We'll talk about both with every finalist.

What you'll do
- Manage design consultants and review plans and specifications.
- Prepare bid packages and run bidding for construction contracts.
- Coordinate with city street departments on paving schedules.
- Report project status to the board each month.

Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in civil engineering.
- Professional Engineer license in Oregon, or able to earn one by comity within six months.
- Eight or more years in water or wastewater design or construction.
- Experience managing consultants and construction contracts.

Preferred
- Experience with state revolving fund or other grant-funded projects.
- Experience with trenchless pipe rehabilitation.

Pay: $118,000 to $142,000 a year.
