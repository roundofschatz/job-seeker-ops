## About

I'm a civil engineer who gets water and sewer projects built on time, and I like the jobs where a deadline and a neighborhood both count. At Harlow Valley Utilities I've taken grant-funded water main projects from design to bid without losing a dollar of grant money, and before that I started our trenchless sewer lining program.

## Experience

### Senior Engineer, Capital Delivery, Harlow Valley Utilities (March 2020 to present)

Nine state revolving fund projects to bid on schedule, $31 million in all. I keep a bid calendar that works back from each grant deadline, and I meet the state's funding officer every month.

### Project Engineer, Harlow Valley Utilities (June 2014 to February 2020)

Started the trenchless sewer lining program in 2017. I wrote the specifications for cured-in-place pipe lining that the utility still uses, and I ran the resident meetings before each street was lined. Complaints per mile of lined pipe ran at about a quarter of what open-trench work drew.
