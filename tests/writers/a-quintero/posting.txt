Clinic Operations Manager
Juniper Ridge Health Partners, Tucson, Arizona. Full time.

Juniper Ridge Health Partners runs three primary care clinics in Tucson: Oracle Road, Grant Road and Valencia. This spring all three clinics add evening hours, Monday through Thursday until 8 p.m. We're hiring a Clinic Operations Manager to run daily operations across the three sites and to bring down patient wait times, which our patients rate as their top complaint.

What you'll do
- Run daily operations for three clinics with 40 staff, including medical assistants, nurses and front desk teams.
- Own the clinics' $6.2 million operating budget and staff schedules.
- Launch and staff evening hours at all three sites.
- Cut the average wait from check-in to seeing a provider, now 34 minutes, and report it monthly to the leadership team.
- Work with the Epic team on scheduling templates and check-in workflows.

Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in nursing, health administration or a related field.
- Five or more years in clinical operations or nurse leadership.
- Experience managing staff schedules and a budget for a unit or clinic.
- Working knowledge of Epic or a similar electronic health record.
- A record of improving patient wait times or throughput.

Preferred
- Fluent in English and Spanish.
- Lean or Six Sigma training.
- Experience opening a new clinic site or service.

Pay: $98,000 to $115,000 a year, plus benefits.
