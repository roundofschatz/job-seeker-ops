Operations Supervisor, Distribution Center
Ironwood Trail Supply Co., Sparks, Nevada. Full time, second shift.

Ironwood Trail Supply opened its new 600,000-square-foot distribution center in Sparks in April. Five months in, we ship 82% of store orders on time against a goal of 97%, and our stores are running short of the gear customers come in for. We're hiring an operations supervisor who has brought a new building up to speed before.

What you'll do
- Supervise a shift of 40 associates in receiving, putaway and picking.
- Run cycle counts and keep inventory accuracy above 99%.
- Build weekly shift schedules and cover call-outs.
- Lead daily start-up meetings and monthly safety audits.
- Report productivity and on-time numbers to the site manager every week.

Requirements
- Three or more years supervising warehouse or distribution associates.
- Experience with a warehouse management system.
- OSHA 30-hour General Industry card.
- Forklift certification, or able to earn it in the first 30 days.
- High school diploma or equivalent.

Preferred
- Experience opening or ramping up a new building.
- Bilingual in English and Spanish.
- Lean or Six Sigma training.

Pay: $68,000 to $78,000 a year.
