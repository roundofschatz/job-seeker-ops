Math Curriculum Coordinator, Grades 6 to 8
Silver Larch School District, Spokane Valley, Washington. Full time, 220-day contract.

Last spring 38% of our 8th graders met the state math standard, down from 46% in 2023. This year our four middle schools adopt a new math curriculum. Our coordinator will lead the middle school math teachers through it and help them use data to catch students before they fall behind.

What you'll do
- Lead the grades 6 to 8 math curriculum adoption across four middle schools.
- Plan and lead professional development for 30 math teachers.
- Build common unit assessments and help teachers use the results.
- Coach new math teachers in their classrooms.
- Report progress to the assistant superintendent each quarter.

Requirements
- Valid Washington teaching certificate with a mathematics endorsement.
- Five or more years teaching middle school math.
- Experience leading professional development for teachers.
- Experience using student assessment data to plan instruction.
- Bachelor's degree in mathematics, education or a related field.

Preferred
- Master's degree in curriculum and instruction or a related field.
- Experience with a curriculum adoption.
- Bilingual in English and Spanish.

Salary: placed on the district's certificated salary schedule, with a stipend for the longer contract.

Application materials written with AI tools will not be considered.
