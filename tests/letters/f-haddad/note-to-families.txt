Cottonwood Springs Middle School, Math Department Note, May 20, 2024

Dear families,

Our 8th graders' math results are in — and they're the best I've seen in this building. This spring 61% of our 8th graders met the state math standard, up from 44% in 2021.

That growth is not just a number, but a sign of what our teachers built together. Every unit now ends with a common test, and every week our teachers meet to look at what those tests show and regroup the students who need a lesson again.

I've chaired the math department for five years now, and I'm proud of this team.

Thank you for everything you do at home.

Nadia Haddad
Math Department Chair
