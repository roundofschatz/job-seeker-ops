Middle School Math Instructional Coach
Juniper Flats School District, Yakima, Washington. Full time, 200-day contract.

This fall we hired 11 new math teachers for our three middle schools, and seven of them are in their first year in a classroom. Our instructional coach will work beside them every week, so their students get a full year of math no matter who's teaching it.

What you'll do
- Coach new math teachers in their classrooms, with weekly visits and follow-up planning.
- Lead a monthly session for all middle school math teachers.
- Help teachers build and use common unit assessments.
- Report on each new teacher's progress to the director of teaching and learning each semester.

Requirements
- Valid Washington teaching certificate with a mathematics endorsement.
- Five or more years teaching middle school math.
- Experience coaching or mentoring teachers.

Preferred
- Master's degree in education or a related field.
- Experience leading professional development.
- Bilingual in English and Spanish.

Salary: placed on the district's certificated salary schedule.
